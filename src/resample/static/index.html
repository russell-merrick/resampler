    Drag a point along its axis. NOTE LENGTH is how long each step is (half notes to 32nds). PATTERN is the idea: lock, leap, reverse, ping-pong, call/response. CLIP WIDTH and OFFSET pick which bars of the sample you steal notes from. PITCH stays in the detected key. DENSITY is how many steps fire — 100% is the full loop, 50% a random half, 10% one random hit. Resample picks a new set. Ctrl+click or double-click a dot to lock it so Resample will not change it.

    🔁 the loop click a cell · drag a note to extend · % row is chance

    Clip width / offset = which notes you can use. Loop length = how long the idea repeats. Density (Resample / the control star) picks how many steps actually fire; the rest stay silent. Click an active note to turn that step off and lower density. Click it again to bring it back. Drag a note right to hold it across steps — the sample plays through instead of retriggering. The % row is extra chance on the steps that remain.

    Melody map — visual only. Angle is pitch class (C at the top). Radius is time in the sample — center is the start, edge is the end. Dashed rings are your clip width / offset window. The path and highlighted star follow the loop; they do not edit it.
